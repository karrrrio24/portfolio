关于 / ABOUT

杨晨欣

YANG CHENXIN

杨晨欣来自福建厦门，2026年毕业于西安美术学院跨媒体艺术系公共艺术专业， 现为该系公共艺术方向硕士研究生。创作多围绕动态装置与新媒体展开， 常从日常物件和个人感受出发，结合光影与互动，营造轻松、有趣且略带荒诞感的空间体验。

Chenxin Yang is from Xiamen, Fujian. She graduated in 2026 from the Public Art program in the Department of Transmedia Art at Xi'an Academy of Fine Arts and is currently pursuing a master's degree in the same field. Her practice focuses on kinetic installation and new media, combining everyday objects, personal experience, light and interaction to create playful spatial experiences with a subtle sense of the absurd.
